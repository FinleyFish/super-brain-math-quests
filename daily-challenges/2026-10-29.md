### 🎓 Math Challenge for 2026-10-29

**Topic**: Multi-step Word Problem
**Problem**: Toan had 447 comic books. He bought 121 more at a fair, but then gave 13 to each of his 3 children. How many comic books does he have left?

**Solution**:
1. Calculate total after buying: 447 + 121 = 568.
2. Calculate total given away: 13 * 3 = 39.
3. Subtract the amount given away from the total: 568 - 39 = 529.

Answer: 529
