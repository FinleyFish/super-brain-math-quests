### 🎓 Math Challenge for 2026-09-22

**Topic**: Long Division
**Problem**: What is 1054 ÷ 14? (Provide the quotient and remainder)

**Solution**:
1. Determine how many times 14 goes into the first digits of 1054.
2. Subtract the result from the dividend to find the remainder.
3. The final quotient is 75 with a remainder of 4.

Answer: 75 remainder 4
