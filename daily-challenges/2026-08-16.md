### 🎓 Math Challenge for 2026-08-16

**Topic**: Migration
**Problem**: Tom has a collection of 128 marbles. He gives half to his sister, and then gives 15 of the remaining marbles to his best friend. How many marbles does Tom have left?
**Solution**: Initial: 128
After giving half to sister: 128 ÷ 2 = 64
After giving some to friend: 64 - 15 = 49
**Tom has 49 marbles left.**
