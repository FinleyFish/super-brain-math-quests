### 🎓 Math Challenge for 2026-09-15

**Topic**: Long Division
**Problem**: What is 2623 ÷ 24? (Provide the quotient and remainder)

**Solution**:
1. Determine how many times 24 goes into the first digits of 2623.
2. Subtract the result from the dividend to find the remainder.
3. The final quotient is 109 with a remainder of 7.

Answer: 109 remainder 7
