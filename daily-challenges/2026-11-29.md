### 🎓 Math Challenge for 2026-11-29

**Topic**: Compound Area
**Problem**: An L-shaped floor is made of two rectangular sections. Section A is 9m by 19m, and Section B is 6m by 7m. What is the total area?

**Solution**:
1. Calculate Area A: 9 * 19 = 171 sq m.
2. Calculate Area B: 6 * 7 = 42 sq m.
3. Add both areas together: 171 + 42 = 213 sq m.

Answer: 213 sq m
