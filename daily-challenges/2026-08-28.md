### 🎓 Math Challenge for 2026-08-28

**Topic**: Compound Area
**Problem**: An L-shaped floor is made of two rectangular sections. Section A is 14m by 18m, and Section B is 11m by 8m. What is the total area?

**Solution**:
1. Calculate Area A: 14 * 18 = 252 sq m.
2. Calculate Area B: 11 * 8 = 88 sq m.
3. Add both areas together: 252 + 88 = 340 sq m.

Answer: 340 sq m
