### 🎓 Math Challenge for 2026-12-08

**Topic**: Compound Area
**Problem**: An L-shaped floor is made of two rectangular sections. Section A is 11m by 19m, and Section B is 10m by 5m. What is the total area?

**Solution**:
1. Calculate Area A: 11 * 19 = 209 sq m.
2. Calculate Area B: 10 * 5 = 50 sq m.
3. Add both areas together: 209 + 50 = 259 sq m.

Answer: 259 sq m
