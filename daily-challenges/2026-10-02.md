### 🎓 Math Challenge for 2026-10-02

**Topic**: Multi-step Word Problem
**Problem**: Toan had 738 rare coins. He bought 67 more at a fair, but then gave 12 to each of his 4 children. How many rare coins does he have left?

**Solution**:
1. Calculate total after buying: 738 + 67 = 805.
2. Calculate total given away: 12 * 4 = 48.
3. Subtract the amount given away from the total: 805 - 48 = 757.

Answer: 757
