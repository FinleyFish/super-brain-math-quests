### 🎓 Math Challenge for 2026-10-31

**Topic**: Long Division
**Problem**: What is 3971 ÷ 43? (Provide the quotient and remainder)

**Solution**:
1. Determine how many times 43 goes into the first digits of 3971.
2. Subtract the result from the dividend to find the remainder.
3. The final quotient is 92 with a remainder of 15.

Answer: 92 remainder 15
