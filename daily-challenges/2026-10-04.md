### 🎓 Math Challenge for 2026-10-04

**Topic**: Long Division
**Problem**: What is 2823 ÷ 37? (Provide the quotient and remainder)

**Solution**:
1. Determine how many times 37 goes into the first digits of 2823.
2. Subtract the result from the dividend to find the remainder.
3. The final quotient is 76 with a remainder of 11.

Answer: 76 remainder 11
