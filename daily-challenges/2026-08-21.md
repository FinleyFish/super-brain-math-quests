### 🎓 Math Challenge for 2026-08-21

**Topic**: Compound Area
**Problem**: An L-shaped floor is made of two rectangular sections. Section A is 12m by 9m, and Section B is 11m by 12m. What is the total area?

**Solution**:
1. Calculate Area A: 12 * 9 = 108 sq m.
2. Calculate Area B: 11 * 12 = 132 sq m.
3. Add both areas together: 108 + 132 = 240 sq m.

Answer: 240 sq m
