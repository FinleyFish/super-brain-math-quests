### 🎓 Math Challenge for 2026-08-21

**Topic**: Multi-step word problems
**Problem**: Sarah has $50. She buys 3 books for $8 each and a backpack for $15. How much money does she have left?
**Solution**: Step 1: Calculate the total cost of the books: 3 × $8 = $24. Step 2: Add the cost of the backpack: $24 + $15 = $39. Step 3: Subtract the total spent from the original amount: $50 - $39 = $11. Answer: $11
