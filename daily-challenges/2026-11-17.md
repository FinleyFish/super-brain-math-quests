### 🎓 Math Challenge for 2026-11-17

**Topic**: Fractions (Advanced)
**Problem**: Emelia is baking a giant cake. The recipe calls for 3/4 cup of sugar for the base and 1/3 cup for the frosting. She only has a 1/12 cup measuring scoop. How many scoops of sugar does she need in total for both the base and the frosting?

**Solution**:
1. Find the total amount of sugar: 3/4 + 1/3.
2. Find a common denominator for 4 and 3, which is 12.
3. Convert the fractions: 3/4 = 9/12 and 1/3 = 4/12.
4. Add the fractions: 9/12 + 4/12 = 13/12.
5. Since each scoop is 1/12, she needs 13 scoops.

Answer: 13 scoops
