**Topic**: Long division with remainders
**Problem**: Divide 1,574 by 12.
**Solution**: Step 1: 15 ÷ 12 = 1 remainder 3. Step 2: Bring down the 7 to make 37. 37 ÷ 12 = 3 remainder 1 (since 12 × 3 = 36). Step 3: Bring down the 4 to make 14. 14 ÷ 12 = 1 remainder 2. Answer: 131 R 2
