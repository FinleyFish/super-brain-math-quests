### 🎓 Math Challenge for 2026-09-09

**Topic**: Long Division
**Problem**: What is 2320 ÷ 33? (Provide the quotient and remainder)

**Solution**:
1. Determine how many times 33 goes into the first digits of 2320.
2. Subtract the result from the dividend to find the remainder.
3. The final quotient is 70 with a remainder of 10.

Answer: 70 remainder 10
