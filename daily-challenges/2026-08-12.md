### 🎓 Math Challenge for 2026-08-12

**Topic**: Migration
**Problem**: James has £40. He spends 1/4 of his money on a new book and 0.2 of his money on a snack. How much money does he have left?
**Solution**: Book: 1/4 of £40 = £10
Snack: 0.2 x £40 = £8
Total spent: £10 + £8 = £18
Remaining: £40 - £18 = £22
**James has £22 left.**
