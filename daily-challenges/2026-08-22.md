### 🎓 Math Challenge for 2026-08-22

**Topic**: Multi-step multiplication and division
**Problem**: A bakery makes 12 trays of cupcakes, with 15 cupcakes on each tray. They sell the cupcakes in boxes of 4. How many full boxes can they make?
**Solution**: Step 1: Find the total number of cupcakes: 12 trays × 15 cupcakes/tray = 180 cupcakes. Step 2: Divide the total by the box size: 180 ÷ 4 = 45. Answer: 45 boxes
