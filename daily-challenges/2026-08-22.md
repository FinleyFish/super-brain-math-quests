### 🎓 Math Challenge for 2026-08-22

**Topic**: Multi-step Word Problem
**Problem**: Toan had 537 rare coins. He bought 136 more at a fair, but then gave 29 to each of his 3 children. How many rare coins does he have left?

**Solution**:
1. Calculate total after buying: 537 + 136 = 673.
2. Calculate total given away: 29 * 3 = 87.
3. Subtract the amount given away from the total: 673 - 87 = 586.

Answer: 586
