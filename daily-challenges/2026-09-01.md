### 🎓 Math Challenge for 2026-09-01

**Topic**: Compound Area
**Problem**: An L-shaped floor is made of two rectangular sections. Section A is 11m by 11m, and Section B is 11m by 4m. What is the total area?

**Solution**:
1. Calculate Area A: 11 * 11 = 121 sq m.
2. Calculate Area B: 11 * 4 = 44 sq m.
3. Add both areas together: 121 + 44 = 165 sq m.

Answer: 165 sq m
