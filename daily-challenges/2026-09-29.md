### 🎓 Math Challenge for 2026-09-29

**Topic**: Multi-step Word Problem
**Problem**: Toan had 481 comic books. He bought 136 more at a fair, but then gave 16 to each of his 5 children. How many comic books does he have left?

**Solution**:
1. Calculate total after buying: 481 + 136 = 617.
2. Calculate total given away: 16 * 5 = 80.
3. Subtract the amount given away from the total: 617 - 80 = 537.

Answer: 537
