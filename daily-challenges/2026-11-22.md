### 🎓 Math Challenge for 2026-11-22

**Topic**: Long Division
**Problem**: What is 4034 ÷ 34? (Provide the quotient and remainder)

**Solution**:
1. Determine how many times 34 goes into the first digits of 4034.
2. Subtract the result from the dividend to find the remainder.
3. The final quotient is 118 with a remainder of 22.

Answer: 118 remainder 22
