### 🎓 Math Challenge for 2026-11-22

**Topic**: Fraction Comparison & Logic
**Problem**: Three friends are sharing a pizza. Alice eats 1/4 of the pizza, Bob eats 3/8, and Charlie eats the rest. Who ate the most pizza, and what fraction did Charlie eat?

**Solution**:
1. Find a common denominator for 1/4 and 3/8, which is 8.
2. Convert Alice's share: 1/4 = 2/8.
3. Bob's share is 3/8.
4. Bob ate more than Alice (3/8 > 2/8).
5. Calculate Charlie's share: 1 - (2/8 + 3/8) = 1 - 5/8 = 3/8.
6. Both Bob and Charlie ate the most.

Answer: Bob and Charlie (3/8 each)
