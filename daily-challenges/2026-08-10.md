### 🎓 Math Challenge for 2026-08-10

**Topic**: Migration
**Problem**: A bakery sells boxes of cookies. Each box contains 24 cookies. If the bakery sells 15 boxes on Monday, how many cookies did they sell in total?
**Solution**: 24 x 15
Step 1: 24 x 10 = 240
Step 2: 24 x 5 = 120
Step 3: 240 + 120 = 360
**Total cookies sold: 360**
