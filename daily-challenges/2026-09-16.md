### 🎓 Math Challenge for 2026-09-16

**Topic**: Long Division
**Problem**: What is 1103 ÷ 35? (Provide the quotient and remainder)

**Solution**:
1. Determine how many times 35 goes into the first digits of 1103.
2. Subtract the result from the dividend to find the remainder.
3. The final quotient is 31 with a remainder of 18.

Answer: 31 remainder 18
