### 🎓 Math Challenge for 2026-11-19

**Topic**: Long Division & Logic
**Problem**: A farmer has 1,458 apples. He wants to pack them into crates that hold 24 apples each. He wants to know how many full crates he will have and how many apples will be left over for his family.

**Solution**:
1. Divide 1,458 by 24.
2. 24 * 60 = 1440.
3. 1458 - 1440 = 18.
4. The quotient is 60 and the remainder is 18.

Answer: 60 crates and 18 apples
