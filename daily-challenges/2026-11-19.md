### 🎓 Math Challenge for 2026-11-19

**Topic**: Multi-step Word Problem
**Problem**: Toan had 618 crystals. He bought 78 more at a fair, but then gave 28 to each of his 6 children. How many crystals does he have left?

**Solution**:
1. Calculate total after buying: 618 + 78 = 696.
2. Calculate total given away: 28 * 6 = 168.
3. Subtract the amount given away from the total: 696 - 168 = 528.

Answer: 528
