### 🎓 Math Challenge for 2026-08-26

**Topic**: Compound Area
**Problem**: An L-shaped floor is made of two rectangular sections. Section A is 12m by 20m, and Section B is 9m by 11m. What is the total area?

**Solution**:
1. Calculate Area A: 12 * 20 = 240 sq m.
2. Calculate Area B: 9 * 11 = 99 sq m.
3. Add both areas together: 240 + 99 = 339 sq m.

Answer: 339 sq m
