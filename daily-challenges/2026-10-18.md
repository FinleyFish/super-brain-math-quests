### 🎓 Math Challenge for 2026-10-18

**Topic**: Compound Area
**Problem**: An L-shaped floor is made of two rectangular sections. Section A is 12m by 13m, and Section B is 12m by 11m. What is the total area?

**Solution**:
1. Calculate Area A: 12 * 13 = 156 sq m.
2. Calculate Area B: 12 * 11 = 132 sq m.
3. Add both areas together: 156 + 132 = 288 sq m.

Answer: 288 sq m
