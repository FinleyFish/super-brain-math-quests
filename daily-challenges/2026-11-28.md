### 🎓 Math Challenge for 2026-11-28

**Topic**: Long Division
**Problem**: What is 1223 ÷ 32? (Provide the quotient and remainder)

**Solution**:
1. Determine how many times 32 goes into the first digits of 1223.
2. Subtract the result from the dividend to find the remainder.
3. The final quotient is 38 with a remainder of 7.

Answer: 38 remainder 7
