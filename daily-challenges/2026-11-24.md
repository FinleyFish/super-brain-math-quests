### 🎓 Math Challenge for 2026-11-24

**Topic**: Multi-step Word Problem
**Problem**: Toan had 274 rare coins. He bought 72 more at a fair, but then gave 23 to each of his 5 children. How many rare coins does he have left?

**Solution**:
1. Calculate total after buying: 274 + 72 = 346.
2. Calculate total given away: 23 * 5 = 115.
3. Subtract the amount given away from the total: 346 - 115 = 231.

Answer: 231
