### 🎓 Math Challenge for 2026-08-14

**Topic**: Migration
**Problem**: Mia is baking a cake. The recipe requires 3/4 of a cup of sugar. She only has a 1/4 cup measure. How many times must she fill the 1/4 cup measure to get the correct amount of sugar? If she then adds another 0.5 cups for frosting, how much sugar did she use in total (as a fraction or decimal)?
**Solution**: Sugar for cake: 3/4 ÷ 1/4 = 3 times.
Total sugar: 3/4 + 0.5
Convert 0.5 to fraction: 1/2 or 2/4.
3/4 + 2/4 = 5/4 = 1 and 1/4 (or 1.25).
**She fills the measure 3 times; total sugar used is 1.25 cups.**
