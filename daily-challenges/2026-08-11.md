### 🎓 Math Challenge for 2026-08-11

**Topic**: Migration
**Problem**: Sarah has a rectangular garden. The length is 8 meters and the width is 5 meters. What is the total area of her garden, and what is its perimeter?
**Solution**: Area = Length x Width
8m x 5m = 40 square meters.

Perimeter = 2 x (Length + Width)
2 x (8m + 5m) = 2 x 13m = 26 meters.
**Area: 40 sq m, Perimeter: 26m**
