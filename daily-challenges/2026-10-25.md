### 🎓 Math Challenge for 2026-10-25

**Topic**: Compound Area
**Problem**: An L-shaped floor is made of two rectangular sections. Section A is 10m by 15m, and Section B is 8m by 7m. What is the total area?

**Solution**:
1. Calculate Area A: 10 * 15 = 150 sq m.
2. Calculate Area B: 8 * 7 = 56 sq m.
3. Add both areas together: 150 + 56 = 206 sq m.

Answer: 206 sq m
