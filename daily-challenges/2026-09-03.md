### 🎓 Math Challenge for 2026-09-03

**Topic**: Compound Area
**Problem**: An L-shaped floor is made of two rectangular sections. Section A is 11m by 8m, and Section B is 10m by 4m. What is the total area?

**Solution**:
1. Calculate Area A: 11 * 8 = 88 sq m.
2. Calculate Area B: 10 * 4 = 40 sq m.
3. Add both areas together: 88 + 40 = 128 sq m.

Answer: 128 sq m
