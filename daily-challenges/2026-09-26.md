### 🎓 Math Challenge for 2026-09-26

**Topic**: Multi-step Word Problem
**Problem**: Toan had 426 rare coins. He bought 135 more at a fair, but then gave 28 to each of his 6 children. How many rare coins does he have left?

**Solution**:
1. Calculate total after buying: 426 + 135 = 561.
2. Calculate total given away: 28 * 6 = 168.
3. Subtract the amount given away from the total: 561 - 168 = 393.

Answer: 393
