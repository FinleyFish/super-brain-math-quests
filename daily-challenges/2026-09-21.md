### 🎓 Math Challenge for 2026-09-21

**Topic**: Multi-step Word Problem
**Problem**: Toan had 331 comic books. He bought 121 more at a fair, but then gave 18 to each of his 3 children. How many comic books does he have left?

**Solution**:
1. Calculate total after buying: 331 + 121 = 452.
2. Calculate total given away: 18 * 3 = 54.
3. Subtract the amount given away from the total: 452 - 54 = 398.

Answer: 398
