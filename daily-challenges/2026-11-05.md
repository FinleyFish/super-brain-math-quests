### 🎓 Math Challenge for 2026-11-05

**Topic**: Multi-step Word Problem
**Problem**: Toan had 774 rare coins. He bought 66 more at a fair, but then gave 11 to each of his 4 children. How many rare coins does he have left?

**Solution**:
1. Calculate total after buying: 774 + 66 = 840.
2. Calculate total given away: 11 * 4 = 44.
3. Subtract the amount given away from the total: 840 - 44 = 796.

Answer: 796
