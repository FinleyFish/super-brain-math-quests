### 🎓 Math Challenge for 2026-10-06

**Topic**: Compound Area
**Problem**: An L-shaped floor is made of two rectangular sections. Section A is 14m by 12m, and Section B is 5m by 7m. What is the total area?

**Solution**:
1. Calculate Area A: 14 * 12 = 168 sq m.
2. Calculate Area B: 5 * 7 = 35 sq m.
3. Add both areas together: 168 + 35 = 203 sq m.

Answer: 203 sq m
