### 🎓 Math Challenge for 2026-12-03

**Topic**: Compound Area
**Problem**: An L-shaped floor is made of two rectangular sections. Section A is 13m by 14m, and Section B is 11m by 7m. What is the total area?

**Solution**:
1. Calculate Area A: 13 * 14 = 182 sq m.
2. Calculate Area B: 11 * 7 = 77 sq m.
3. Add both areas together: 182 + 77 = 259 sq m.

Answer: 259 sq m
