### 🎓 Math Challenge for 2026-11-20

**Topic**: Compound Area (Subtraction)
**Problem**: A rectangular garden measures 15m by 10m. In the center, there is a circular pond with a diameter of 4m (use 3.14 for pi). What is the remaining area of the garden available for planting?

**Solution**:
1. Calculate the total garden area: 15 * 10 = 150 sq m.
2. Calculate the radius of the pond: 4 / 2 = 2m.
3. Calculate the area of the pond: 3.14 * 2 * 2 = 12.56 sq m.
4. Subtract the pond area from the garden area: 150 - 12.56 = 137.44 sq m.

Answer: 137.44 sq m
