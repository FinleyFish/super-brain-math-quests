### 🎓 Math Challenge for 2026-11-15

**Topic**: Multi-step Word Problem
**Problem**: Toan had 692 crystals. He bought 127 more at a fair, but then gave 23 to each of his 3 children. How many crystals does he have left?

**Solution**:
1. Calculate total after buying: 692 + 127 = 819.
2. Calculate total given away: 23 * 3 = 69.
3. Subtract the amount given away from the total: 819 - 69 = 750.

Answer: 750
