### 🎓 Math Challenge for 2026-11-18

**Topic**: Multi-digit Multiplication & Budgeting
**Problem**: A school is buying new tablets. Each tablet costs $245. The school wants to buy 38 tablets. However, the supplier offers a discount of $15 off each tablet if they buy more than 25. What is the total cost for the tablets after the discount?

**Solution**:
1. Calculate the discounted price per tablet: $245 - $15 = $230.
2. Multiply the discounted price by the number of tablets: 230 * 38.
3. Break down the multiplication: (230 * 30) + (230 * 8).
4. 6900 + 1840 = 8740.

Answer: $8,740
