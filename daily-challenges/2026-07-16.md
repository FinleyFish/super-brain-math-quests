### 🎓 Math Challenge for 2026-07-16

**Problem:**
A rectangular garden is 8 meters long and 5 meters wide. What is the perimeter of the garden, and what is its area?

**Solution:** 

1. Calculate Perimeter (sum of all sides).
2. - Perimeter = 2 * (length + width)
3. - Perimeter = 2 * (8 + 5) = 2 * 13 = 26 meters.
4. Calculate Area (length times width).
5. - Area = length * width
6. - Area = 8 * 5 = 40 square meters.

Answer: Perimeter is 26m, Area is 40 sq m.
