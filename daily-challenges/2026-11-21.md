### 🎓 Math Challenge for 2026-11-21

**Topic**: Compound Area
**Problem**: An L-shaped floor is made of two rectangular sections. Section A is 10m by 8m, and Section B is 10m by 11m. What is the total area?

**Solution**:
1. Calculate Area A: 10 * 8 = 80 sq m.
2. Calculate Area B: 10 * 11 = 110 sq m.
3. Add both areas together: 80 + 110 = 190 sq m.

Answer: 190 sq m
