### 🎓 Math Challenge for 2026-11-21

**Topic**: Multi-step Logic
**Problem**: Toan is organizing a soccer tournament. There are 12 teams. Each team has 11 players and 2 substitutes. He needs to provide one jersey for every player and substitute. Jerseys cost $18 each, but he gets a 10% discount on the total order because he is the coach. What is the final cost?

**Solution**:
1. Calculate players per team: 11 + 2 = 13.
2. Calculate total jerseys needed: 12 * 13 = 156.
3. Calculate total cost before discount: 156 * 18 = 2808.
4. Calculate 10% discount: 2808 * 0.10 = 280.80.
5. Subtract discount from total: 2808 - 280.80 = 2527.20.

Answer: $2,527.20
