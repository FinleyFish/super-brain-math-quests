### 🎓 Math Challenge for 2026-11-25

**Topic**: Long Division
**Problem**: What is 2225 ÷ 24? (Provide the quotient and remainder)

**Solution**:
1. Determine how many times 24 goes into the first digits of 2225.
2. Subtract the result from the dividend to find the remainder.
3. The final quotient is 92 with a remainder of 17.

Answer: 92 remainder 17
