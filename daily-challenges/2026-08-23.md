### 🎓 Math Challenge for 2026-08-23

**Topic**: Compound Area
**Problem**: An L-shaped floor is made of two rectangular sections. Section A is 13m by 15m, and Section B is 6m by 6m. What is the total area?

**Solution**:
1. Calculate Area A: 13 * 15 = 195 sq m.
2. Calculate Area B: 6 * 6 = 36 sq m.
3. Add both areas together: 195 + 36 = 231 sq m.

Answer: 231 sq m
