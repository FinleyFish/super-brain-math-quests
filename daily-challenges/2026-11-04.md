### 🎓 Math Challenge for 2026-11-04

**Topic**: Multi-step Word Problem
**Problem**: Toan had 791 comic books. He bought 106 more at a fair, but then gave 27 to each of his 6 children. How many comic books does he have left?

**Solution**:
1. Calculate total after buying: 791 + 106 = 897.
2. Calculate total given away: 27 * 6 = 162.
3. Subtract the amount given away from the total: 897 - 162 = 735.

Answer: 735
