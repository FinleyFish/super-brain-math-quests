### 🎓 Math Challenge for 2026-09-20

**Topic**: Compound Area
**Problem**: An L-shaped floor is made of two rectangular sections. Section A is 8m by 14m, and Section B is 6m by 9m. What is the total area?

**Solution**:
1. Calculate Area A: 8 * 14 = 112 sq m.
2. Calculate Area B: 6 * 9 = 54 sq m.
3. Add both areas together: 112 + 54 = 166 sq m.

Answer: 166 sq m
