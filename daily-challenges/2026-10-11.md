### 🎓 Math Challenge for 2026-10-11

**Topic**: Compound Area
**Problem**: An L-shaped floor is made of two rectangular sections. Section A is 20m by 9m, and Section B is 7m by 6m. What is the total area?

**Solution**:
1. Calculate Area A: 20 * 9 = 180 sq m.
2. Calculate Area B: 7 * 6 = 42 sq m.
3. Add both areas together: 180 + 42 = 222 sq m.

Answer: 222 sq m
