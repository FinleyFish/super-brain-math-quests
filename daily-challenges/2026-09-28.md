### 🎓 Math Challenge for 2026-09-28

**Topic**: Compound Area
**Problem**: An L-shaped floor is made of two rectangular sections. Section A is 13m by 9m, and Section B is 11m by 9m. What is the total area?

**Solution**:
1. Calculate Area A: 13 * 9 = 117 sq m.
2. Calculate Area B: 11 * 9 = 99 sq m.
3. Add both areas together: 117 + 99 = 216 sq m.

Answer: 216 sq m
