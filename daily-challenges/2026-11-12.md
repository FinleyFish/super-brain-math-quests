### 🎓 Math Challenge for 2026-11-12

**Topic**: Long Division
**Problem**: What is 3064 ÷ 28? (Provide the quotient and remainder)

**Solution**:
1. Determine how many times 28 goes into the first digits of 3064.
2. Subtract the result from the dividend to find the remainder.
3. The final quotient is 109 with a remainder of 12.

Answer: 109 remainder 12
