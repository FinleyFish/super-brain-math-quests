### 🎓 Math Challenge for 2026-11-26

**Topic**: Long Division
**Problem**: What is 1019 ÷ 20? (Provide the quotient and remainder)

**Solution**:
1. Determine how many times 20 goes into the first digits of 1019.
2. Subtract the result from the dividend to find the remainder.
3. The final quotient is 50 with a remainder of 19.

Answer: 50 remainder 19
