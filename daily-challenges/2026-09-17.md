### 🎓 Math Challenge for 2026-09-17

**Topic**: Long Division
**Problem**: What is 811 ÷ 22? (Provide the quotient and remainder)

**Solution**:
1. Determine how many times 22 goes into the first digits of 811.
2. Subtract the result from the dividend to find the remainder.
3. The final quotient is 36 with a remainder of 19.

Answer: 36 remainder 19
