### 🎓 Math Challenge for 2026-09-24

**Topic**: Long Division
**Problem**: What is 1229 ÷ 30? (Provide the quotient and remainder)

**Solution**:
1. Determine how many times 30 goes into the first digits of 1229.
2. Subtract the result from the dividend to find the remainder.
3. The final quotient is 40 with a remainder of 29.

Answer: 40 remainder 29
