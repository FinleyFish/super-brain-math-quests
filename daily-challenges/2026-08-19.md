### 🎓 Math Challenge for 2026-08-19

**Topic**: Compound area of combined rectangles
**Problem**: An L-shaped garden consists of two rectangles. The first is 8m × 4m. The second, which attaches to the side, is 3m × 2m. What is the total area?
**Solution**: Step 1: Calculate the area of the first rectangle: 8m × 4m = 32 sq m. Step 2: Calculate the area of the second rectangle: 3m × 2m = 6 sq m. Step 3: Add the two areas together: 32 + 6 = 38 sq m. Answer: 38 square meters
