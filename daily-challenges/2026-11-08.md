### 🎓 Math Challenge for 2026-11-08

**Topic**: Long Division
**Problem**: What is 1903 ÷ 42? (Provide the quotient and remainder)

**Solution**:
1. Determine how many times 42 goes into the first digits of 1903.
2. Subtract the result from the dividend to find the remainder.
3. The final quotient is 45 with a remainder of 13.

Answer: 45 remainder 13
