### 🎓 Math Challenge for 2026-12-09

**Topic**: Compound Area
**Problem**: An L-shaped floor is made of two rectangular sections. Section A is 8m by 19m, and Section B is 10m by 6m. What is the total area?

**Solution**:
1. Calculate Area A: 8 * 19 = 152 sq m.
2. Calculate Area B: 10 * 6 = 60 sq m.
3. Add both areas together: 152 + 60 = 212 sq m.

Answer: 212 sq m
