### 🎓 Math Challenge for 2026-10-05

**Topic**: Multi-step Word Problem
**Problem**: Toan had 703 rare coins. He bought 134 more at a fair, but then gave 16 to each of his 6 children. How many rare coins does he have left?

**Solution**:
1. Calculate total after buying: 703 + 134 = 837.
2. Calculate total given away: 16 * 6 = 96.
3. Subtract the amount given away from the total: 837 - 96 = 741.

Answer: 741
