### 🎓 Math Challenge for 2026-08-15

**Topic**: Migration
**Problem**: A train leaves the station at 10:15 AM and arrives at its destination at 1:45 PM. How long was the journey in hours and minutes?
**Solution**: From 10:15 AM to 12:15 PM is 2 hours.
From 12:15 PM to 1:15 PM is 1 hour (Total 3 hours).
From 1:15 PM to 1:45 PM is 30 minutes.
**The journey took 3 hours and 30 minutes.**
