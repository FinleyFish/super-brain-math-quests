### 🎓 Math Challenge for 2026-10-17

**Topic**: Multi-step Word Problem
**Problem**: Toan had 623 rare coins. He bought 114 more at a fair, but then gave 22 to each of his 6 children. How many rare coins does he have left?

**Solution**:
1. Calculate total after buying: 623 + 114 = 737.
2. Calculate total given away: 22 * 6 = 132.
3. Subtract the amount given away from the total: 737 - 132 = 605.

Answer: 605
