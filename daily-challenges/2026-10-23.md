### 🎓 Math Challenge for 2026-10-23

**Topic**: Compound Area
**Problem**: An L-shaped floor is made of two rectangular sections. Section A is 19m by 9m, and Section B is 9m by 6m. What is the total area?

**Solution**:
1. Calculate Area A: 19 * 9 = 171 sq m.
2. Calculate Area B: 9 * 6 = 54 sq m.
3. Add both areas together: 171 + 54 = 225 sq m.

Answer: 225 sq m
