### 🎓 Math Challenge for 2026-09-12

**Topic**: Multi-step Word Problem
**Problem**: Toan had 587 comic books. He bought 141 more at a fair, but then gave 19 to each of his 5 children. How many comic books does he have left?

**Solution**:
1. Calculate total after buying: 587 + 141 = 728.
2. Calculate total given away: 19 * 5 = 95.
3. Subtract the amount given away from the total: 728 - 95 = 633.

Answer: 633
