### 🎓 Math Challenge for 2026-10-08

**Topic**: Compound Area
**Problem**: An L-shaped floor is made of two rectangular sections. Section A is 14m by 19m, and Section B is 4m by 10m. What is the total area?

**Solution**:
1. Calculate Area A: 14 * 19 = 266 sq m.
2. Calculate Area B: 4 * 10 = 40 sq m.
3. Add both areas together: 266 + 40 = 306 sq m.

Answer: 306 sq m
