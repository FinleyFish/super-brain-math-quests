### 🎓 Math Challenge for 2026-10-07

**Topic**: Multi-step Word Problem
**Problem**: Toan had 286 rare coins. He bought 108 more at a fair, but then gave 14 to each of his 5 children. How many rare coins does he have left?

**Solution**:
1. Calculate total after buying: 286 + 108 = 394.
2. Calculate total given away: 14 * 5 = 70.
3. Subtract the amount given away from the total: 394 - 70 = 324.

Answer: 324
