### 🎓 Math Challenge for 2026-10-19

**Topic**: Long Division
**Problem**: What is 2514 ÷ 28? (Provide the quotient and remainder)

**Solution**:
1. Determine how many times 28 goes into the first digits of 2514.
2. Subtract the result from the dividend to find the remainder.
3. The final quotient is 89 with a remainder of 22.

Answer: 89 remainder 22
