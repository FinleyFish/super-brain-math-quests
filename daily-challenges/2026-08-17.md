### 🎓 Math Challenge for 2026-08-17

**Topic**: Multi-step Word Problem
**Problem**: Toan had 307 comic books. He bought 44 more at a fair, but then gave 14 to each of his 3 children. How many comic books does he have left?
**Solution**: Total after buying: 307 + 44 = 351
Total given away: 14 * 3 = 42
Final count: 351 - 42 = 309
