### 🎓 Math Challenge for 2026-09-13

**Topic**: Long Division
**Problem**: What is 2900 ÷ 26? (Provide the quotient and remainder)

**Solution**:
1. Determine how many times 26 goes into the first digits of 2900.
2. Subtract the result from the dividend to find the remainder.
3. The final quotient is 111 with a remainder of 14.

Answer: 111 remainder 14
