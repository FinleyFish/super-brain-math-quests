### 🎓 Math Challenge for 2026-08-20

**Topic**: Adding fractions with different denominators
**Problem**: Calculate 2/3 + 1/4.
**Solution**: Step 1: Find a common denominator for 3 and 4, which is 12. Step 2: Convert 2/3 to twelfths: (2 × 4) / (3 × 4) = 8/12. Step 3: Convert 1/4 to twelfths: (1 × 3) / (4 × 3) = 3/12. Step 4: Add the numerators: 8/12 + 3/12 = 11/12. Answer: 11/12
