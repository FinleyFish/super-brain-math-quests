### 🎓 Math Challenge for 2026-10-13

**Topic**: Multi-step Word Problem
**Problem**: Toan had 494 comic books. He bought 75 more at a fair, but then gave 11 to each of his 3 children. How many comic books does he have left?

**Solution**:
1. Calculate total after buying: 494 + 75 = 569.
2. Calculate total given away: 11 * 3 = 33.
3. Subtract the amount given away from the total: 569 - 33 = 536.

Answer: 536
