### 🎓 Math Challenge for 2026-11-02

**Topic**: Long Division
**Problem**: What is 844 ÷ 26? (Provide the quotient and remainder)

**Solution**:
1. Determine how many times 26 goes into the first digits of 844.
2. Subtract the result from the dividend to find the remainder.
3. The final quotient is 32 with a remainder of 12.

Answer: 32 remainder 12
