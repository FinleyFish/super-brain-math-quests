### 🎓 Math Challenge for 2026-09-10

**Topic**: Compound Area
**Problem**: An L-shaped floor is made of two rectangular sections. Section A is 8m by 8m, and Section B is 11m by 11m. What is the total area?

**Solution**:
1. Calculate Area A: 8 * 8 = 64 sq m.
2. Calculate Area B: 11 * 11 = 121 sq m.
3. Add both areas together: 64 + 121 = 185 sq m.

Answer: 185 sq m
