### 🎓 Math Challenge for 2026-10-22

**Topic**: Long Division
**Problem**: What is 4044 ÷ 35? (Provide the quotient and remainder)

**Solution**:
1. Determine how many times 35 goes into the first digits of 4044.
2. Subtract the result from the dividend to find the remainder.
3. The final quotient is 115 with a remainder of 19.

Answer: 115 remainder 19
