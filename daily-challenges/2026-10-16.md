### 🎓 Math Challenge for 2026-10-16

**Topic**: Long Division
**Problem**: What is 2180 ÷ 21? (Provide the quotient and remainder)

**Solution**:
1. Determine how many times 21 goes into the first digits of 2180.
2. Subtract the result from the dividend to find the remainder.
3. The final quotient is 103 with a remainder of 17.

Answer: 103 remainder 17
