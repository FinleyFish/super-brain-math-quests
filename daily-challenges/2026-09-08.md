### 🎓 Math Challenge for 2026-09-08

**Topic**: Compound Area
**Problem**: An L-shaped floor is made of two rectangular sections. Section A is 20m by 11m, and Section B is 6m by 7m. What is the total area?

**Solution**:
1. Calculate Area A: 20 * 11 = 220 sq m.
2. Calculate Area B: 6 * 7 = 42 sq m.
3. Add both areas together: 220 + 42 = 262 sq m.

Answer: 262 sq m
